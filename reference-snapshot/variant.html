QUESTIONS / ANSWERED
What exactly is shielded?+

ShieldTX separates your main wallet from your public trading trail: positions, order history, PnL, liquidation levels and the account used to trade.

Can activity still be seen onchain?+

Yes. Deposits, temporary accounts, solver activity and withdrawals remain visible. ShieldTX shields the direct public association between your funding wallet and the trades.

Do I still trade on Hyperliquid?+

Yes. Your orders route to Hyperliquid’s orderbook through a fresh trading account.

Is ShieldTX the same speed as Hyperliquid?+

Shielding adds a few seconds before an order reaches Hyperliquid’s orderbook.

What markets are supported?+

Every Hyperliquid perpetual market.

Does shielding affect my fills?+

Orders use Hyperliquid’s liquidity. Shielding adds no spread; any ShieldTX fees are separate.

Who controls my funds?+

Funds sit in an Arbitrum smart contract. For the complete custody and verification details, read the ShieldTX trust model ↗.

Is this a mixer?+

No. ShieldTX is a trading tool that isolates perpetual-trade execution data.
